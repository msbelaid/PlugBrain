PlugBrain puts a quick math problem between you and your distracting apps.

Pick the apps that eat your time and choose how many minutes you get before a break. When the time is up, a challenge appears over the app. Solve it to keep going, or tap Leave App and get on with your day. You can also be challenged as soon as a distracting app opens.

The difficulty follows your habits. Each challenge you solve makes the next one harder. Stay away and it eases off again, down to the level you started from: Beginner, Intermediate or Expert. More than 40 kinds of problems cover the range, from simple additions to multi-digit multiplications.

Features

- Math challenges over the apps you choose
- Difficulty that rises with use and drops when you stay away
- Optional challenge when an app opens
- Light and dark theme, 11 languages

Private by design

- No ads, no account, no analytics
- No internet permission: nothing leaves your phone
- Free and open source (GPL-3.0)

Permissions

PlugBrain uses usage access, display over other apps and the Accessibility service to see which app is open and show the challenge on top. During a challenge it also blocks calculators. It only reads the name of the open app, never what is on your screen or what you type.
